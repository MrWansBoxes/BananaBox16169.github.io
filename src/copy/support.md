## Support the team

Banana Box runs on community support, and every contribution goes straight into our
robots and our outreach. You can support us through our
[GoFundMe](https://www.gofundme.com/f/bananabox2026), and we fundraise year-round
through concession stands and recycled parts.

We also launched **Mission FTC-Possible** after NH FIRST lost its main sponsor:
local companies can purchase a table at NH FTC events (scrimmages, qualifiers, and
the state championship) to showcase their products and services to the FIRST
community.

**What sponsors get:** a spot across our social media (Instagram, YouTube), a place
on the banner we carry at the FIRST World Championship, and connections with
motivated STEM students. **What it funds:** robot parts, competition and travel
expenses, and our workshops and outreach.

## Mentorship

We mentor **12 teams** across FIRST Lego League and FIRST Tech Challenge — five
rookie FLL teams at the Londonderry YMCA, three FLL teams in Windham, and four NH FTC
teams. We help them grow robotics skills (CAD, mechanics, programming,
troubleshooting), cognitive skills (problem solving, planning), and collaboration
through teamwork and Gracious Professionalism. To grow our own mentor base, we reach
out to FLL, FTC, and FRC alumni as well as parents and their peers.

To get involved as a sponsor, mentor, or partner, [get in touch](mailto:ftcbananabox16169@gmail.com).
