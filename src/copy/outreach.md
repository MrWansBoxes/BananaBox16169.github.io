## Sustainability plan

We keep the team thriving year over year through six pillars: **team continuity**
(experienced members mentor newcomers and we stay connected with alumni),
**knowledge transfer** (engineering notebooks, CAD libraries, and code templates),
**recruitment** (engaging youth and professionals through outreach), **funding
stability** (fundraising through concessions, recycled parts, and Mission
FTC-Possible), **resource stability** (an organized, well-maintained, safe
workspace), and **succession** (setting SMART goals each season and improving our
processes).
